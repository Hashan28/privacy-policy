Privacy Policy

Page Auto Poster · Last updated: September 24, 2026

Page Auto Poster ("the App") is a private automation tool operated by Hashan. It is used only to publish scheduled posts (images and text) to a Facebook Page that the operator owns and manages. This policy explains what information the App uses and how it is handled.

1. Information we collect

The App only uses the minimum information needed to publish posts:

  • The Facebook Page ID and Page name of the operator's own Page.
  • A Page access token that allows the App to create posts on that Page.
  • The content of the posts the App publishes (text and image links).

The App does not collect, read or store personal information of Page followers, visitors or any other Facebook users. It does not read messages, comments or profile data.

2. How we use information

The information above is used only to create and publish posts on the operator's Facebook Page at scheduled times. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Post text may be generated with an AI text service, and images may be sourced from a free stock photo service. No personal data of Facebook users is sent to these services.

4. Data storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely in an encrypted credential store on a private server controlled by the operator. Access is limited to the operator.

5. Data retention

A simple log of published posts may be kept for record keeping. Access tokens are kept only while the App is in use and are deleted when the App is no longer used.

6. Data deletion

The App does not store data about Facebook users. If you believe the App holds any information related to you and you want it deleted, email pasindudhanawardana@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

You can also remove the App's access at any time from your Facebook account under Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with the updated date shown above.

9. Contact

For any questions about this policy, contact pasindudhanawardana@gmail.com.
